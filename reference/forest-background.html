The detached-leaf check

  1. Pause movement. A gap that remains is already in the drawing.
  2. Align the base. Each leaf meets its stem; each cluster meets its branch.
  3. Resume movement. The joint should stay fixed while the tip swings.

Anime.js changes positions or angles over time. It does not automatically attach one shape to another.

Five useful words

Raster / JPEG
An image stored as a grid of colored pixels, used here for the forest photograph.
Vector / SVG
A drawing described by shapes and paths, used here for the foreground tree.
Group
Shapes that receive a transformation together, like a stem and its leaves.
Base
The point where a leaf or twig should meet its supporting stem or branch.
Pivot
The point that stays fixed while a shape rotates. A swaying twig’s pivot belongs at its base.
