# Portfolio

The language of a programming and photography portfolio organized around a journey up a tree.

## Language

**Tree ascent**:
The visitor's upward journey through the portfolio, beginning at a tree's roots and ending at its treetop.
_Avoid_: Dive, descent

**Roots**:
The introductory stage at the foot of the portfolio's tree ascent, where the visitor first arrives.

**Trunk**:
The stage introducing the portfolio owner, their skills, and their freelance website services.

**Branches**:
The stage showcasing the portfolio owner's programming projects.

**Canopy**:
The stage showcasing the portfolio owner's photography.

**Treetop**:
The final stage of the portfolio's tree ascent, inviting prospective clients to make contact.

**Project**:
A piece of programming work showcased in the portfolio, such as an application, tool, or game.
_Avoid_: Work (when referring specifically to a programming project)

**Photograph**:
An individual photographic work presented as part of the portfolio owner's personal creative showcase.

**Prospective client**:
A visitor considering commissioning paid freelance website work from the portfolio owner.
